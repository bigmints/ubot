Meet your concierge

A little more presence. A little less being everywhere.

Youbot is a personal AI concierge for the people who message you. Give it context, choose what it can do, and let it help with conversations while you stay in control.

How it works

  1. Make it yours

    Set your concierge’s name, personality, useful information and boundaries.

  2. Connect your AI and channels

    Bring an AI provider or a local Ollama model. Connect the messaging services you want to use.

  3. Share your concierge

    Use your generated visitor link, then review conversations and approval requests from your owner dashboard.

Self-hosted today

Version 1 runs on your computer. Keep it awake and Youbot running for replies and scheduled work. The software is free; an online AI provider may charge for usage. Managed cloud hosting is coming soon.

Your visitor link opens a conversation with your concierge. Your owner dashboard is a separate, password-protected workspace.
